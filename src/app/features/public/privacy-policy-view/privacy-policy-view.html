Privacy Policy

QuoteDesks Messenger ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of data processed through our WhatsApp CRM and Meta Graph API platform.

1. Data We Collect

When you register and connect your Meta WhatsApp Business Account to QuoteDesks Messenger, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, work email address, agency name, and user seat credentials.
  • Meta WABA Metadata: WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID), Phone Number ID, and system user tokens.
  • Contact Directories: Customer phone numbers, names, and tags uploaded or synchronized into QuoteDesks Messenger.
  • Message Analytics: Sent, delivered, and read receipt metrics for marketing broadcasts.

2. How We Use Data

We use processed data strictly to deliver QuoteDesks Messenger services:

  • To route and display inbound/outbound WhatsApp conversations in your Shared Team Inbox.
  • To execute authorized WhatsApp marketing broadcast campaigns.
  • To enforce multi-tenant Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) data walling across sales agents.
  • To trigger Gemini AI copilot response suggestions when assigned.

3. Data Non-Retention & Security Standards

QuoteDesks Messenger does not sell, lease, or monetize your customer contacts or conversation logs. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256). All Meta Graph API access complies with Meta Platform Terms.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account data or revoke Meta WABA permissions at any time. Refer to our Data Deletion Instructions page or contact support at admin@quotedesks.com.
