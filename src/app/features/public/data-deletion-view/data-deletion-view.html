Meta Platform Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

QuoteDesks Messenger is a certified Meta Business Integration. In compliance with Facebook Platform Terms, you have full control over your connected Meta accounts and data retention.

How to Delete Your Data & Revoke Meta Authorization

1

Option A: Disconnect via QuoteDesks Messenger Settings

Log in to your QuoteDesks Messenger account, go to Manage -> Integrations -> WhatsApp / Meta Hub, and click Disconnect WABA Account. This immediately clears your access tokens and revokes real-time webhook listeners.

2

Option B: Revoke Authorization Directly via Facebook Settings

Go to your Facebook Profile -> Settings & Privacy -> Settings -> Business Integrations. Locate QuoteDesks Messenger and click Remove. Facebook will automatically dispatch an automated Data Deletion Callback request to our server.

3

Option C: Submit a Formal Data Wipe Request

Send an email to admin@quotedesks.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Our compliance team will permanently purge your contact directory, agency records, and conversation logs within 24 hours.

Automated Meta Callback Response

When Meta dispatches a data deletion request to our callback endpoint (https://messengerapi.quotedesks.com/api/v1/auth/facebook/data-deletion), our system generates a unique confirmation code and status URL allowing you to track data purge completion.
