PART B: LEGAL FRAMEWORK GDPR & DPDP ACT 2023

Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Governing the processing of personal data in connection with Quote Desks platform services.

DPA NAVIGATION

Data Protection Desk

For DPA signing, SCC requests, or security assessment queries:

dpo@quotedesks.com

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Quote Desks Terms of Service and governs the processing of personal data in connection with the services, where applicable, under the GDPR, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), and other applicable data protection laws.

CLAUSE 1

1. Definitions and Roles

Data Controller / Data Fiduciary

The customer or tenant using Quote Desks (“Tenant”) determines the purposes and means of processing personal data and acts as the Data Controller under applicable GDPR provisions and, where applicable, as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act.

Data Processor

Quote Desks processes personal data on behalf of the Tenant in accordance with documented instructions and the applicable agreement.

CLAUSE 2

2. Scope and Purpose Limitation

Quote Desks will process personal data only for purposes necessary to provide the contracted services, including CRM management, customer communication, quotation generation, itinerary management, messaging, automation, and related platform functions.

Strict Monitization Prohibition: Quote Desks will not sell or monetize customer personal data for advertising purposes.
CLAUSE 3

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Quote Desks will retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, comply with contractual requirements, maintain security, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Deletion Protocol: Upon termination of the applicable services or a valid deletion request, Quote Desks will delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, subject to legal, security, backup, and regulatory requirements.

Note: Where technically required, residual copies in backups may remain temporarily until they are securely overwritten in accordance with applicable retention procedures.

CLAUSE 4

4. Security Measures and Logging

Quote Desks implements appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including, where applicable:

Encryption in transit & at rest
Access controls & authentication
Role-based access controls (RBAC)
System isolation & monitoring
Data masking mechanisms
Automated activity logging

Security measures may be updated periodically to address evolving technical and security risks.

CLAUSE 5

5. Breach Responsibilities and Notification

In the event of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting personal data processed on behalf of a Tenant, Quote Desks will notify the affected Tenant without undue delay after becoming aware of the breach, where required by applicable law.

Where reasonably necessary, Quote Desks will provide available information concerning the nature of the incident, affected systems or data, and mitigation measures.

Tenant Responsibility: The Tenant remains responsible for determining and making any regulatory, customer, or Data Principal notifications that it is legally required to make, while Quote Desks will provide reasonable cooperation and assistance.
CLAUSE 6

6. Sub-processors and International Data Transfers

Sub-processors: The Tenant authorizes Quote Desks to engage appropriate third-party service providers and sub-processors, including cloud hosting, communication, analytics, AI, payment, and infrastructure providers, where necessary to provide the services. Quote Desks will require applicable sub-processors to maintain appropriate contractual and data protection obligations.

Data Transfers: Where personal data is transferred internationally, Quote Desks will use appropriate safeguards required under applicable data protection laws, which may include Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

CLAUSE 7

7. Data Principal / Data Subject Rights

Where applicable, Quote Desks will provide reasonable assistance to the Tenant in responding to valid requests concerning personal data, including requests for:

  • Access: Reviewing processed personal data records.
  • Correction: Updating inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion / Erasure: Purging data from platform records.
  • Restriction of Processing: Limiting specific processing scope.
  • Data Portability: Transferring data in machine-readable formats.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Revoking previously granted authorizations.
  • Other Statutory Rights: Exercising rights under GDPR or DPDP Act.

The Tenant remains responsible for responding to Data Principals or Data Subjects and determining whether a request is legally valid.

CLAUSE 8

8. Confidentiality

Quote Desks will ensure that persons authorised to process personal data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and access personal data only to the extent necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

CLAUSE 9

9. Compliance and Cooperation

Quote Desks will provide reasonable cooperation and information necessary for the Tenant to assess compliance with applicable data protection obligations relating to the services, subject to reasonable confidentiality, security, and operational requirements.

CLAUSE 10

10. Governing Agreement

This DPA should be read together with the Quote Desks Terms of Service and applicable Privacy Policy. In the event of a conflict relating specifically to the processing of personal data, the applicable data protection provisions of the DPA will govern to the extent required by applicable law.
