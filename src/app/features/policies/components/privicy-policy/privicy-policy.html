SECTION 1

About Quote Desks and Quote Desks Messenger

Quote Desks is a multi-tenant travel CRM and quotation management platform designed for travel agencies, DMCs, tour operators, and travel professionals. It helps businesses manage leads, customers, travel quotations, itineraries, communications, workflows, and related travel operations through a centralized platform.

Quote Desks Messenger is a communication and engagement service within the Quote Desks ecosystem. It enables businesses to communicate with their customers through supported channels such as WhatsApp, SMS, and email, including customer notifications, transactional communications, marketing communications, and other business messages.

Quote Desks and Quote Desks Messenger may integrate with third-party services, including communication platforms, cloud infrastructure providers, payment services, analytics services, and AI or technology providers, to deliver and support these services.

SECTION 2

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Quote Desks (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, processes, and protects personal data through Quote Desks and Quote Desks Messenger.

We may process personal data on behalf of travel agencies, DMCs, tour operators, and other business customers. We aim to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Compliance Standard: All data workflows operate in alignment with standard multi-tenant cloud isolation protocols and international privacy practices.
SECTION 3

Our Role as Processor and Controller

Depending on the circumstances, Quote Desks operates under two distinct legal capacities:

Data Processor

Processing customer data on behalf of our business customers for messaging, quotations, itineraries, CRM operations, and related services.

Data Controller / Fiduciary

Processing information directly provided by our business customers for account management, service delivery, platform security, and platform improvement.

SECTION 4

Information We Collect

We process the following categories of information to maintain platform functionality:

  • Contact Details: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and communication preferences.
  • Travel & Quotation Data: Travel preferences, itinerary details, travel quotations, and booking-related information.
  • Communication Logs: Messages and communication data submitted through WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, SMS, email, or other integrated services.
  • Technical Metadata: Platform identifiers, timestamps, preferred language, timezone, and device/browser info.
  • Security & Diagnostics: System logs and diagnostic information required for platform security, troubleshooting, and service maintenance.
Sensitive Data Note: We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless it is voluntarily provided by users or strictly required for a specific travel booking service.
SECTION 5

Lawful Basis and Purpose

We process personal data where permitted by applicable law, on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: Explicit authorization granted by users or business accounts.
  • Contract Performance: Necessary for delivering requested quotations, travel itineraries, and CRM features.
  • Legitimate Business Interests: Maintaining platform stability, preventing fraud, and optimizing workflows.

Data is used strictly to provide services, process quotations and itineraries, facilitate communications, maintain user accounts, improve platform functionality, prevent misuse, and satisfy regulatory obligations.

SECTION 6

WhatsApp, Meta, SMS and Email Data

Messaging Integrations: Messages routed via third-party channels strictly follow provider privacy guidelines, including Meta’s Business Messaging Policies.

Where Quote Desks Messenger integrates with third-party communication platforms such as WhatsApp, Meta services, SMS providers, or email services, relevant data may be processed through those platforms in accordance with their respective privacy policies and service terms.

SECTION 7

Data Sharing and International Transfers

Personal data may be shared under strict operational safeguards with:

  • Business customers utilizing Quote Desks.
  • Verified communication, cloud hosting, payment gateway, analytics, and AI technology service providers.
  • Government or regulatory authorities where legally mandated by court order or statute.

Where applicable, international data transfers are protected through appropriate legal safeguards, including standard contractual protections or recognized adequacy mechanisms.

SECTION 8

Data Retention, Security and Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required by statutory law.

We employ multi-layered technical and organizational security measures, including end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-tenant system isolation, and continuous threat monitoring.

Where applicable, users or business customers may request deletion of personal data, subject to legal and contractual compliance requirements.
