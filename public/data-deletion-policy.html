Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: 2026-08-08

At Pace Messenger, we respect your right to privacy and your right to control your personal data. This Data Deletion Policy outlines the procedures for the deletion of user data in compliance with relevant data protection regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA).

1. Right to Erasure

Users of Pace Messenger have the right to request the deletion of their personal data from our systems. This includes account information, contact lists, and message history.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your data, you must submit a written request via email to our support team at support@pacetravels.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • A clear statement requesting the deletion of your data.

3. Deletion Process

Upon receiving a valid data deletion request:

  1. Verification: We will verify the identity of the person making the request to ensure they are the authorized account holder.
  2. Processing: Once verified, we will initiate the deletion of your personal data from our active databases, including CRM contacts, messaging logs, and template data.
  3. Timeline: The deletion process will be completed within 30 days of the verified request.
  4. Confirmation: We will notify you via email once the deletion process has been successfully completed.

4. Exceptions

Please note that we may retain certain data if it is necessary for legitimate business purposes or to comply with our legal obligations. This may include:

  • Transactional records for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Data required for security, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.
  • Data stored in routine system backups, which will be overwritten or deleted in accordance with our standard backup retention cycle (typically 90 days).

5. Third-Party Services

For data processed via third-party services integrated into Pace Messenger (e.g., WhatsApp APIs), we will ensure that deletion requests are cascaded to those third parties where technically feasible and required by law.
